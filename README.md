# OLA Ride Performance Analytics Dashboard
This repository contains an end-to-end data analytics project evaluating the operational and financial performance of OLA ride-hailing services in Bengaluru. The project encompasses raw data extraction, SQL-based data transformation, and the development of an interactive business intelligence dashboard to uncover actionable insights regarding ride volume, revenue channels, and cancellation bottlenecks.   🛠️ Tech StackDatabase & Transformation: SQL (MySQL)   Data Visualization: Power BI   Dataset: 1-month transactional data, 100,000 rows   📊 Dataset OverviewThe analysis is based on a generated 1-month dataset of 100,000 ride records for Bengaluru city. Key attributes include:   Booking Details: Date, Time, Booking ID, Booking Status, Customer ID.   Vehicle Types: Auto, Prime Plus, Prime Sedan, Mini, Bike, eBike, Prime SUV.   Operational Metrics: Pickup/Drop Locations, Avg VTAT (Vehicle Turnaround Time), Avg CTAT (Customer Turnaround Time), Ride Distance.   Financials & Feedback: Booking Value, Payment Method, Driver Ratings, Customer Ratings.   🗄️ SQL Data ProcessingTo optimize reporting performance and keep data retrieval clean, dedicated SQL VIEWS were engineered for all major KPIs. Key analytical queries include:   SUCCESSFULL_BOOKINGS: Filtered view of all 63,967 successfully completed rides.   AVERAGE_DISTANCE_PER_VEHICLE_TYPE: Aggregated distance metrics revealing Prime Sedans cover the highest average distance at 15.76 km.   CANCELLED_RIDES_BY_CUSTOMERS & CANCELLED_BY_DRIVERS_P_C_ISSUES: Segregated cancellation data to isolate 10,499 customer cancellations and 6,542 driver cancellations caused by personal or car issues.   UPI_PAYMENTS: Extracted 25,881 payments made specifically via UPI.   INCOMPLETE_BOOKINGS: Analyzed partial rides to identify "Customer Demand" (1,601 occurrences) and "Vehicle Breakdown" (1,591 occurrences) as leading failure points.   📈 Interactive DashboardThe visualization layer features an app-like dark mode interface with neon green branding and a dynamic left-hand navigation panel.   Dashboard Views:Overall: Top-level KPIs including Total Bookings (103,024), Total Booking Value (35M), and dynamic Ride Value Over-time trends.   Vehicle Type: Performance breakdown by fleet category, tracking Total Distance Travelled and Success Booking Values.   Revenue: Tracking of income by payment method, highlighting Cash and UPI as the dominant transaction types, alongside a Top 5 Customers leaderboard.   Cancellation: Detailed breakdowns of exact cancellation reasons for both drivers (e.g., Personal & Car related issues at 35.49%) and customers (e.g., Driver not moving towards pickup location at 30.24%).   Ratings: Matrix view of Average Rating by Drivers and Customers across all vehicle types, showing a highly consistent ~4.0 average score across the board.   🚀 Key Business InsightsFleet Utilization: Prime Sedans dominate long-distance trips (15.76 km average), while lower-tier vehicles maintain high volume.   Revenue Streams: Digital payments have massive adoption with nearly 26,000 successful UPI transactions, though cash remains a heavily utilized primary channel.   Service Bottlenecks: The overall cancellation rate sits at 28.08%. The most significant operational friction point is drivers failing to move toward the pickup location, causing over 30% of customer-initiated cancellations.
